# Travel Policy — Cóndor Logística (fictional company)

Employees who travel to a client site must request approval from their manager at least five business days before the trip. Trips shorter than one day do not need advance approval.

Domestic flights are booked in economy class. Business class is allowed only for international flights longer than six hours.

The daily allowance for meals is 120,000 COP in Colombian cities and 60 USD abroad. Receipts are required for any single expense above 50,000 COP.

Hotel bookings must use the corporate travel portal. If the portal has no availability, the employee may book directly and must attach the confirmation to the expense report.
