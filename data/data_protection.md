# Personal Data Protection Policy — Cóndor Logística (fictional company)

Customer personal data may only be processed for the purpose the customer authorized. Every new use of personal data requires a documented authorization.

Customers may request access, correction or deletion of their data at any time. The company must answer these requests within 15 business days.

Personal data must never be pasted into external AI tools. Approved internal tools must remove identifiers such as names, ID numbers, emails and phone numbers before sending text to a language model.

The data protection officer is Laura Medina, reachable at dpo@condor-logistica.example. Incidents involving personal data must be reported to her within 24 hours of being detected.
