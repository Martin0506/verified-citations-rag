# Remote Work Policy — Cóndor Logística (fictional company)

Employees may work remotely up to two days per week after completing their probation period. Teams that serve clients on site may set a different schedule with the area manager.

The company provides a laptop and reimburses up to 80,000 COP per month for home internet, with the provider's invoice.

Remote employees must be reachable on the team chat between 9:00 and 16:00, Bogotá time.
