# Expense Reimbursement Policy — Cóndor Logística (fictional company)

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 calendar days after the expense. Reports submitted later are reimbursed only with written approval from the finance director.

Reimbursements are paid in the next payroll cycle after approval. Approved reports are processed twice a month, on the 15th and on the last business day.

Alcohol, traffic fines and personal entertainment are never reimbursed.

Questions about a specific report go to the finance desk at finanzas@condor-logistica.example or 310 555 0142.
